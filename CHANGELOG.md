# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- A tap on the tile opens a card with every station, every fuel, its address and whether it is open.
- One cell with two or three fuels chosen shows a row per fuel under the station's name.
- An arrow beside a price that changed: up in red, down in green.
- New top bar item **Cheap fuel**: "Diesel 1,759 · JET" while the cheapest price of a fuel is at a limit or under.
  Choose the template sensor in the plugin's details, and switch it on, the fuel and the limit on the screen.

## 0.1.0
- First version: E5, E10 and diesel at up to ten stations you pick, sorted, filtered and coloured the way you choose.
